# Vision

## One line

The best tool for turning long **Bangla, Hindi and English** videos into short clips, running on
your own computer, with your own AI.

## The problem

- Creators in Bangladesh and India record long videos (podcasts, lectures, waz, tech talks,
  coaching, interviews, streams) and need daily Shorts/Reels to grow.
- Cutting clips by hand takes hours. Cloud tools charge per minute of source video, which hurts
  people with long videos, and most of them handle Bangla and Hindi badly or not at all: weak
  clip selection, broken conjuncts in captions, English-only titles.
- Prices built for the US market don't fit creators paid at South Asian CPM rates.

## Who it's for (in priority order)

1. **Bangla creators** on Facebook and YouTube: talk shows, podcasts, education, religious talks,
   tech and freelancing channels. Often mix Bangla and English in speech ("Banglish").
2. **Hindi creators** with the same formats, often mixing Hindi and English ("Hinglish").
3. **English creators** who want unlimited local clipping without credits.
4. Small agencies and editors who clip for several of the above.

## Principles

1. **Languages done right.** Bangla, Hindi and English are first-class. Every feature is tested in
   all three. A feature that works only in English isn't done.
2. **Local first, honest about the cloud.** Everything can run offline. If the user picks a cloud
   AI, we say exactly what leaves the computer (transcript text, never the video).
3. **The user reviews before anything is exported.** We suggest clips; the creator decides.
4. **Their own content.** The product is built for creators clipping their own videos.
5. **Works on ordinary PCs.** A laptop without a GPU must finish a 1-hour video, even if slower.

## How we'll know it works

- **Main metric:** share of generated clips that creators actually post. Target: at least 40%
  per language (independent tests of the market leader on English content land around 40–60%).
- A 1-hour video finishes on a mid-range PC (6–8 GB VRAM GPU) in under 20 minutes end to end.
- At least 15 creators (5 per language) use it for 4 weeks during validation (M6).

## Not in v1

- Full timeline video editor, B-roll, AI dubbing, voice cloning
- Direct posting to platforms (export files + ready-to-paste titles instead)
- macOS and Linux (Windows x64 first)
- Languages beyond bn, en, hi
- UI translated into Bangla/Hindi (planned for M6; strings are kept translatable from the start)

## Monetization (to validate in M6, not built yet)

Free tier with watermark and limits; one-time or yearly Pro license priced for Bangladesh and
India, payable with local methods (bKash/Nagad, UPI). Agencies as a higher tier.
